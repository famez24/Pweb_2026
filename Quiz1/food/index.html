Taste of Surabaya

Local Food

Surabaya's local food reflects the city's bold flavours, rich culinary traditions, and diverse cultural influences.

Rujak Cingur

Rujak cingur is a traditional Surabayan dish made with fruits, vegetables, tofu, tempeh, and sliced cow snout. It is served with a thick sauce made from petis, peanuts, and chili. The combination creates a unique sweet, savory, and spicy flavor.

Lontong Balap

Lontong balap is a traditional street food from Surabaya made with rice cakes, bean sprouts, fried tofu, and lentho. It is served with a savory sauce and usually topped with fried onions and sambal. The dish has a simple yet flavorful combination of textures and tastes.

Rawon

Rawon is a traditional East Javanese beef soup with a distinctive dark black broth. The broth is made with kluwek, giving it a rich, savory, and slightly nutty flavor. It is commonly served with rice, bean sprouts, and salted egg.

Sate Klopo

Sate klopo is a traditional Surabayan satay made from beef or chicken coated with grated coconut. The meat is grilled until the coconut becomes fragrant and slightly crispy. It has a savory, slightly sweet, and distinctive coconut flavor.

Tahu Tek

Tahu tek is a traditional Surabayan dish made from fried tofu, potatoes, bean sprouts, and sometimes eggs. The ingredients are cut into small pieces and covered with a thick peanut and petis sauce. It has a savory, sweet, and slightly spicy flavor.
