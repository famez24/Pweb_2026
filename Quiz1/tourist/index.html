Explore Surabaya

Tourist Places

Surabaya offers historic landmarks, peaceful green spaces, and memorable places to experience the character of the city.

Tugu Pahlawan

Tugu Pahlawan is one of Surabaya's most iconic landmarks and a symbol of Indonesia's struggle for independence. It has a tall monument surrounded by a green open area. Visitors can also explore the nearby Museum Sepuluh Nopember to learn about the history of the Battle of Surabaya.

Surabaya Zoo

Surabaya Zoo is a popular tourist destination featuring various species of animals. It is a suitable place for families and visitors who enjoy nature and wildlife. The zoo also provides an educational experience about different animals.

Surabaya North Quay

Surabaya North Quay is a waterfront destination located near the Port of Tanjung Perak. Visitors can enjoy views of the sea, ships, and the surrounding harbor area. It is also a popular spot for watching the sunset.

Kota Tua Surabaya

Kota Tua Surabaya is a historic area filled with old buildings and colonial-era architecture. Visitors can explore the streets while learning about the city's history and heritage. The area is also a popular spot for photography and cultural exploration.

Kalimas Boat Tour

Kalimas Boat Tour allows visitors to explore Surabaya along the historic Kalimas River. During the trip, visitors can enjoy views of the river, surrounding buildings, and several city landmarks. It offers a different way to experience the atmosphere of Surabaya.
